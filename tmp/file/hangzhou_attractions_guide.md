# Hangzhou Top Attractions Guide

A concise, practical reference for tourists — including locations and transportation options.

---

## 🌟 West Lake (Xī Hú)
- **📍 Location**: Central Hangzhou, straddling Shangcheng & Xihu Districts; core area includes Broken Bridge, Su Causeway, and Leifeng Pagoda.
- **🚌 Transport**:
  - Metro: Line 1 → Longxiangqiao Station (Exit B, 5-min walk to Hubin area); or Line 7 → Wuhan Road Station (for Xiling Bridge).
  - Bus: Routes Y2, Y3, Y9, 51, 52, 118 — stop at "West Lake Scenic Area" or "Su Causeway".
  - Bike: Hellobike/Daibu stations abundant around lake perimeter.

## 🏯 Lingyin Temple (Língyǐn Sì)
- **📍 Location**: Northwest of West Lake, in Lingyin Road, Xihu District.
- **🚌 Transport**:
  - Metro: Line 1 → Longxiangqiao → transfer to Bus Y2/Y12 (20 min) OR Line 1 → Xianghu Station → Bus 7/807 (15 min).
  - Bus: Direct Y2, Y12, 7, 807 → "Lingyin Temple" stop.
  - Taxi/Ride-hailing: ~15 mins from West Lake downtown.

## 🌿 Xixi National Wetland Park (Xīxī Shīdì Gōngyuán)
- **📍 Location**: Northwestern Hangzhou, Xihu & Xihu (Yuhang) border; main entrance at Zhoujia Village.
- **🚌 Transport**:
  - Metro: Line 14 → Xixi Wetland Station (Exit A, 3-min walk to South Entrance).
  - Bus: 356, 375, Y13 → "Xixi Wetland Park".
  - Free shuttle buses operate inside park between entrances.

## 🏮 Leifeng Pagoda (Léifēng Tǎ)
- **📍 Location**: Southern shore of West Lake, near Nanping Mountain, Xihu District.
- **🚌 Transport**:
  - Metro: Line 1 → Huanglong Sports Center → Bus Y6/Y9 (10 min) OR Line 7 → Wuhan Road → 5-min walk.
  - Bus: Y6, Y9, 4, 31 → "Leifeng Pagoda" stop.
  - Walkable (~20 min) south from West Lake’s Hubin area.

## 🏮 Hefang Street (Héfāng Jiē)
- **📍 Location**: Historic pedestrian street in Shangcheng District, east of West Lake.
- **🚌 Transport**:
  - Metro: Line 7 → Wuhan Road Station (Exit C, 3-min walk) — *most convenient*.
  - Bus: 8, 12, 25, Y2 → "Hefang Street" or "Wushan Square".
  - Walkable (~10 min) from West Lake’s Qingbo Gate.

## 🚢 Grand Canal (Hangzhou Section)
- **📍 Location**: Runs through Gongshu & Xiacheng Districts; highlights: Qiantang River Bridge, Guangqi Bridge, Shantang Street.
- **🚌 Transport**:
  - Metro: Line 5 → Dajiang Road / Gongchen Bridge / Shangtang Road Stations.
  - Bus: 2, 10, 26, Y6 → stops near Shantang Street or Gongchen Bridge.
  - Boat tours available from Wulin Port (Line 1 → Wulin Square → 5-min walk).

---
ℹ️ **General Tips**:
- **Metro App**: Download "Hangzhou Metro" or use Alipay/WeChat Mini Programs for real-time maps & QR code entry.
- **Buses**: Use Alipay “Bus Code” or physical Yikatong card (sold at metro stations).
- **Taxis**: Start at ¥13 (first 3 km); Didi widely used.
- **Bikes**: Hellobike & Meituan bikes cover >95% of scenic zones.
